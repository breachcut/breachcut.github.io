How it works

Three steps. That's it.

Install & sign in

Open BreachcutSetup from your downloads. It installs everything by itself, then sign in with Discord. That's the whole setup.

Play Siege

Breachcut records in the background, sees every “+100 Kill”, saves your multi-kills and starts editing.

Post it

Open the TikTok or YouTube tab, grab your finished video and upload. Done.

FAQ

Questions

Is Breachcut free?

Yes, totally free.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

That shows for new apps that aren't from the Microsoft Store. Click More info, then Run anyway. The setup downloads Breachcut straight from its public GitHub page.

Do I need Medal or OBS?

No. Breachcut records by itself. It watches the score feed on your screen, so when “+100 Kill” pops up it knows you got a kill and saves the play.

Can I get banned for using it?

Breachcut never reads or touches the game. Its recorder captures the screen and your speakers the same way OBS does, and kill detection only looks at the picture, so there's nothing for BattlEye to flag.

Windows says the file is unknown. Is it safe?

Breachcut is new, so Windows hasn't seen it much yet. The installer is a plain script (install.ps1) you can open in Notepad to see exactly what it does: it sets up Python, Pillow and FFmpeg, then copies Breachcut into your AppData folder. All the code is on GitHub.

Why sign in with Discord?

It saves your name and profile picture so Breachcut feels like yours. We only see your Discord name, picture and app version. Never your clips.

How do updates work?

Breachcut updates itself when you open it. You can switch that off in Settings → General.
